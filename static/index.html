      Common questions

      What is an ATS?

      An Applicant Tracking System is software companies use to sort resumes by keywords and format before a recruiter reads them.

      How is the score calculated?

      60% keyword match with the job description, 15% for having the standard resume sections, and 25% for quality checks like contact info, links, length and measurable results. 70% or more is a strong match, 45–69% is decent, and below 45% needs work.

      Should I add every missing keyword?

      No. Only add skills you truly have, in the right place such as your skills section or project bullets. Never list skills you can’t back up in an interview.

      Why can’t it read my PDF?

      Scanned or image-only PDFs have no readable text. Export your resume as a PDF from Word or Google Docs, or paste the text instead.

      Does a high score guarantee an interview?

      No. It’s a guide to improve your resume for a specific job, not a hiring decision.
